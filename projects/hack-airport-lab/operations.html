    Broken Access Control: what is happening?
    
      
    In plain language: signing in proves who a user is; it does not grant permission to use every feature. The fare editor is an operator action. In the vulnerable demo, a customer session can submit a fare change because the handler does not verify the user's role.
    
      
    Impact in this demo: a customer can change a fictional route's displayed fare, affecting the integrity of the booking data. No real airline price or payment is changed.
    
      
    How to fix it: enforce authorization in the server-side handler for every request. Require an operator role for fare changes, deny by default, and never rely on hiding an admin page or button in the browser.
    
      
    Sources: OWASP Authorization Cheat Sheet · OWASP Top 10: Broken Access Control · CWE-862
